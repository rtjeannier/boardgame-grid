Generated by `python -m pipeline.build --report`. This is the reference every
later change is compared against — record both datasets when you regenerate it,
and say in the commit which numbers moved and why.

Taken at the tip of `feat/contract-and-interactive-grid`, before any change to
the model or its configuration.

`data/cache/` and `boardgames_ranks.csv` are all gitignored, so these numbers
cannot be regenerated from a clean checkout — which is exactly why they are
written down here.

## Agreement with the recorded history

Four of the five figures carried in commit-message bodies reproduce exactly,
which is what gives confidence these are the same measures rather than merely
plausible ones:

| Measure | Recorded | Computed | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cohesion, per axis | 3.11x null | 3.11x | exact |
| Corpus null | 0.125 | 0.125 | exact |
| Median pick rank | 267 | 267 | exact |
| Slots filled | 175/175 | 175/175 | exact |
| Canary lost | Terraforming Mars | Terraforming Mars | exact |
| Name-truth | ~81% | 83% | close |

Name-truth is measured **per axis**, not per spoke — a spoke sums several axes
and is named after one of them, so spoke-level name-truth asks the wrong
question and reads 66%. This is the measure that needed
`FeatureSpace.axis_names`, a field nothing else reads: it was added for this
and the measure was never written.
